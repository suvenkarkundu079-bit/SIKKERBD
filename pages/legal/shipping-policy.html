We work exclusively with premier parcel logistics networks across Bangladesh including Steadfast Courier, Pathao Courier, and RedX. Upon order dispatch from our Banani warehouse, you will receive an automated tracking code via SMS and WhatsApp.

3. 5-Ply Breakage-Free Cushioning

Drinkware is fragile, so we never skimp on protection:

  • Each item is wrapped in thick bubble barrier film.
  • Individual pieces are secured in custom foam die-cut compartments.
  • The master carton is crafted from heavy 5-ply corrugated kraft board taped with 'FRAGILE — HANDLE WITH CARE' security tape.

4. Inspection on Delivery

You are welcome to inspect the outer shipping box in the presence of the courier rider before paying for Cash on Delivery. If the outer box is crushed or punctured, please open and verify the contents immediately.
