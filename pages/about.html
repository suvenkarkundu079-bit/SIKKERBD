SIKKER OVERVIEW

SIKKER is a specialized Bangladeshi e-commerce brand delivering high-grade ceramic, porcelain, and heat-resistant borosilicate glassware. We fuse minimalist Scandinavian design principles with functional thermal ergonomics to elevate quiet tea and coffee moments.

FOUNDATION

Our Story

Founded with a commitment to quality and tactile excellence, SIKKER emerged to address a clear gap in the local market: access to durable, heat-safe, and thoughtfully sculpted drinkware. Every collection undergoes rigorous thermal testing and quality control before reaching homes and workspaces across the nation.

PURPOSE

Our Mission

To craft and deliver premium glass and ceramic drinkware engineered for longevity, heat retention, and aesthetic harmony, backed by protective packaging and prompt nationwide fulfillment.

ASPIRATION

Our Vision

To establish SIKKER as South Asia's leading design-first drinkware brand, setting standard benchmarks for material integrity, customer experience, and modern tableware aesthetics.
