Frequently Asked Questions

How long does delivery take within Bangladesh?

Orders in Dhaka take 24–48 hours. Delivery outside Dhaka takes 2–4 business days via reliable courier services.

Are SIKKER double-wall glasses safe for hot coffee and tea?

Yes. All our double-wall glass cups are crafted from high-grade borosilicate glass designed to handle boiling liquids up to 150°C without cracking.

What is your policy for damaged items during delivery?

We pack every order securely in multi-layered protective wrapping. If an item arrives broken, contact us on WhatsApp with pictures within 24 hours for a full replacement.
